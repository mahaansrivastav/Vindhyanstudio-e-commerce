The Developer wrote the Vindhyan Studio e-commerce software: the website, admin panel, server application, database design and related documentation, including future updates made by the Developer ("the **Software**").

The Developer owns all copyright and other intellectual property in the Software. Nothing in this agreement transfers ownership to the Business. Hosting the Software on the Business's accounts (domain, Netlify, Render, Supabase, GitHub or others) does not transfer ownership.

The Developer gives the Business a **free of charge**, non-exclusive, non-transferable licence to run the Software to operate its own online store at vindhyanstudio.in.

Without the Developer's written permission, the Business will not, and will not allow anyone else to:

- give, sell or show the source code to any other developer, agency or company;

The Business owns its brand, logo, domain, product photos and descriptions, prices, customer and order data, and its service accounts. The Developer will not use the Business's data or brand for any other purpose.

The Developer may update and maintain the Software when reasonable. There is no fixed obligation to provide support, and no fee is payable under this agreement.

## 7. If the Developer stops working on the project
If either party wants to end the arrangement:
- the Business may keep using the **current version** for ____ days so it can move to another solution, and may export all its own data; and
- the Business may get a separate licence for another developer to maintain the Software **only with a new written agreement** with the Developer.

## 8. Ending the licence
The Developer may end this licence by written notice (email is enough) if the Business breaks section 4. Otherwise either party may end it with 30 days' written notice. When it ends, the Business stops using the Software after the period in section 7 and deletes any copies of the source code it holds.

## 9. Confidentiality
The Business keeps the source code and technical details of the Software confidential.

The Software is provided "as is". The Developer is not liable for loss of business, data or profit arising from its use.
